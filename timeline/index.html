Timeline

Everything I’ve worked on, in order.

  1. 2022–2023

    Dropshipping

    Online store

    My first attempt. I was 17, about to finish high school, and wanted to sell things online. It went nowhere.

    To take payments you needed a company. I didn’t know you could set up an LLC in the US, and even if I had, I didn’t have the money for it. I had no bank account, and Stripe didn’t work in Jordan.

    After high school, everyone around me went to university. I took a gap year instead to figure out what I actually wanted to do.

  3. 2023

    Twitter

    Writing and digital products

    During the gap year I started writing on Twitter. Before AI made it easy, I grew the account to about 10k followers.

    I sold digital products and Notion templates to that audience. One template made $3.5k in a single day. That money paid for everything that came next.

  5. 2023–2025

    cFlow

    Marketing agency

    I started an agency from my bedroom, first as Don Advertising, then Don Marketing, cold-calling construction companies in the US. We ran their ads on Google, Facebook and Instagram. Most clients would call a lead once or twice and give up, so we started qualifying leads ourselves and had appointment setters book them straight into the client’s calendar. Clients got better results and stayed longer, at $1,500–3,000 a month.

    Churn was still the hardest part, so I built a bigger offer: a business’s whole marketing setup, from the website and SEO to ads, CRM and appointment setting, for a one-time $8–12k fee plus commission. We had the case studies, but for a local business that’s a lot to put down upfront, and it didn’t sell the way we wanted. Our long-term retainer clients stayed with us the whole time.

  7. 2025

    Annualize

    Startup

    I was paying a lot for software. Annual plans are often 20–40% cheaper, but you have to pay the whole year upfront. Annualize covered that invoice, so startups could get the annual price without paying it all at once.

    We applied to YC several times and talked to a lot of founders. It became clear it would take a long time before it made money, and not all of us were ready to build something like that. After about six months, we moved on.

  9. 2025–2026

    Aurify

    Local SEO for home service companies

    After a short break, still looking after our long-term clients, we flipped the agency model. Instead of a high monthly retainer for leads, Aurify takes care of what every local business should have and rarely does: their website, SEO, Google Business Profile and reviews.

    getaurify.com ↗

  11. 2026–now

    Nihilo

    An AI agent your customers can text

    Running SMS and email campaigns for our clients, I kept seeing texts win. I wanted an agent that lived inside those texts and could handle things like booking appointments. I’d been using an AI agent myself and liked it, so we built one that lives in iMessage. Hardly anyone else was doing it.

    Nihilo gives e-commerce and B2C brands one phone number their customers can text on iMessage, RCS or SMS. The agent answers questions, takes orders and brings customers back, and we build and run it for them. It’s what I’m working on now.
